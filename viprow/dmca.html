DMCA Policy

This document explains our adherence to the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and outlines our procedures for resolving copyright infringement claims.

Copyright Stance & Media Hosting

We highly value the intellectual property of creators and expect our visitors to do the same. This platform functions entirely as a search directory and link aggregator. We provide event schedules and route viewers to external third-party streaming platforms.

We absolutely do not host, stream, upload, or store any video media on our own network. All broadcasts and files are controlled and hosted exclusively by independent external providers.

Submitting a Takedown Notice

If you discover a link on our platform that routes users to copyrighted material without authorization, we strongly advise contacting the actual third-party video host first, as they are the ones who can physically remove the media from the internet.

However, if you need a specific link deleted from our directory, please send us an official DMCA notice containing the following details:

  • Your complete name, physical address, phone number, and email address.
  • A precise description of the copyrighted work that has allegedly been infringed.
  • The exact URL(s) on our website where the infringing link is located.
  • A statement affirming your good-faith belief that the material is being used without permission from the copyright owner, their agent, or the law.
  • A declaration, under penalty of perjury, that the details in your claim are factual and that you are the copyright holder or their authorized representative.
  • Your physical or digital signature.

Filing a Counter-Notification

If you suspect a link was deleted from our directory due to an error or misidentification, you can file a counter-claim. Your submission must include:

  • Your full contact details (name, address, phone number, and email).
  • A clear description of the removed material and exactly where it previously appeared on our site.
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, confirming your good-faith belief that the removal was a mistake.
  • Your consent to the jurisdiction of your local federal court (or a recognized competent court if you reside outside the United States).
  • Your physical or digital signature.

Repeat Infringer Policy

Following DMCA guidelines, we retain the right to block, suspend, or ban visitors who repeatedly violate our copyright rules. Additionally, external streaming sources that receive consistent, verified infringement reports will be permanently blacklisted and scrubbed from our directory.

Contact Details

To file a DMCA claim, submit a counter-notice, or ask a copyright-related question, please reach out through our Contact Page or send an email directly to contact@. To ensure your request is routed and handled quickly, please make sure to use "DMCA Notice" as your email subject line.
